# Site catalog

A working catalog of websites delivered by Beyond Web Co. Entries are based on the live-site content provided or verified on 27 October 2026. This catalog records the site's business purpose and delivered scope; it is not a source-of-truth for commercial terms, project status, or legal claims.

## 1. Hayagriva Civil Academy

- **Repository:** `beyondwebco-lgtm/Hayagriva`
- **Live site:** https://hayagriva-seven.vercel.app
- **Category:** Education / Civil-services exam preparation
- **Location served:** Mandya, Karnataka; online learners across India
- **Site purpose:** A lead-generation and learning-program portal for aspirants preparing for UPSC, KPSC, State PSC, and other government-job examinations.

### What the site communicates

Hayagriva Civil Academy presents a concept-first preparation model supported by daily current-affairs analysis, smart exam strategy, one-to-one mentorship, answer-writing guidance, revision materials, and mock tests. It frames the academy around the “Learn • Lead • Serve” message and the founder/mentor's preparation experience since 2015.

### Programs and content

- UPSC Foundation Course with Prelims, Mains, Interview, test-series, and mentorship coverage
- KPSC KAS preparation, including Karnataka-specific content and Kannada/English support
- Daily current-affairs program, mapped to the exam syllabus
- Subject modules for polity, history, geography, economy, environment, science and technology, and ethics
- Skill and exam-preparation modules including essay writing, answer writing, NCERT foundation, PYQ discussion, mock tests, and revision series

### Main website functions

- Program discovery and course browsing
- Course-specific enrolment calls to action
- Mentor-led positioning and student testimonials
- Social-channel discovery for YouTube, WhatsApp, Telegram, and other learning communities
- Direct WhatsApp enquiry flow for prospective learners

- **Location:** Gandipet, Hyderabad
- **Site purpose:** A high-end residential project showcase and private-sales enquiry experience for a lakeside luxury development.

- Interactive estate-plan and residence-layout exploration
- Amenities presentation, including Club Vega and 75+ lifestyle facilities
