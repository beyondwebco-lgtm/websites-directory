## Detailed Description

The site positions Vega Towers as a 24-acre lakeside estate in Gandipet with six residential towers, privacy-focused large-format homes, amenities, residence layouts, and buyer consultation flows. It targets high-net-worth buyers evaluating 4 BHK, 5 BHK, and penthouse residences.

## Key Features

- Project philosophy, master-plan, gallery, and location content
- Residence and floor-plan exploration
- Bespoke configuration and price-estimate presentation
- Amenities and lifestyle showcase
- Brochure, price-breakup, and private-preview calls to action
- 3D walkthrough promotion
