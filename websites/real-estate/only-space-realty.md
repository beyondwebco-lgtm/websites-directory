## Detailed Description

The site presents Only Space Realty's founder-led advisory services across Shilaj, Sindhu Bhavan Road, SG Highway, and surrounding West Ahmedabad corridors. It covers verified residential, commercial, and land opportunities, with a focus on legal transparency, local market insight, price negotiation, and transaction support.

## Key Features

- Residential, commercial, and land-investment service pages
- West Ahmedabad location and corridor content
- Founder consultation and enquiry calls to action
- Four-step property-advisory process
- Client-review and trust content
- Price-breakup and site-consultation entry points
